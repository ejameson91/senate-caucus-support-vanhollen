Export your contact details and current message for the optional desktop helper. It supports the contact forms for Senators Gillibrand, King, Cortez Masto, Padilla, Murphy, Schatz, Gallego, Schiff, Klobuchar, Kim, Alsobrooks, Luján, Sanders, Schumer, Van Hollen, Coons, Booker, Markey, Slotkin, Warren, Peters, Reed, Rosen, Shaheen, Merkley, Fetterman, Hickenlooper, Ossoff, Blunt Rochester, and Hassan (the ten newest forms require desktop version 1.1.0 or later).
