# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for contributors and users. Participate respectfully, including when discussing politics or reviewing work.

- Treat others with dignity. Critique ideas and changes, not people's identities or character.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, intimidate, or make unwanted sexual comments or advances toward anyone.
- Do not publish someone else's private information without their permission.
- Respect boundaries and accept constructive feedback. Avoid spam and disruptive behavior.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [ejameson2010@gmail.com](mailto:ejameson2010@gmail.com). Maintainers will review reports, protect privacy as reasonably possible, and decide on an appropriate response. Please include enough information to understand what happened; do not post sensitive details publicly.
