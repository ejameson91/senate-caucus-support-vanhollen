Senate Form Helper

Imports the contact export from the website. Your details stay in memory while this app is open. The export file remains on disk until you delete it.

The helper supports Senators Gillibrand, King, Cortez Masto, Padilla, Murphy, Schatz, Gallego, Schiff, Klobuchar, Kim, Alsobrooks, Luján, Sanders, Schumer, and Van Hollen. Other offices are not yet supported. Offices may limit responses to constituents.

First open “share your opinion on legislation” for Cortez Masto, choose “Share your opinion or comments on bills or other issues” for Padilla, choose a topic on Murphy's page, or open “Share your Opinion” for Van Hollen. For Murphy, use that same topic in the helper or leave it to be chosen on the form. For Schatz, answer the response question yourself; his form does not have a subject field and does not offer every territory. Sanders's form has no subject field and limits comments to 2,000 characters. If a prefix is unavailable, choose it manually. Filling sends your details to the form page. Review all fields, complete any CAPTCHA, and submit yourself. The helper never submits forms.

Import an export to begin.
