Senate Form Helper

Imports the contact export from the website. Your details stay in memory while this app is open. The export file remains on disk until you delete it.

The helper supports Senators Gillibrand, King, and Cortez Masto. Other offices are not yet supported. Offices may limit responses to constituents.

For Cortez Masto, first open “share your opinion on legislation” on her page. If a prefix is unavailable, choose it manually. Filling sends your details to the form page. Review all fields, complete any CAPTCHA, and submit yourself. The helper never submits forms.

Import an export to begin.
