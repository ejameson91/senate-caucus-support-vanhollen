Senate Form Helper

Imports the contact export from the website. Your details stay in memory while this app is open. The export file remains on disk until you delete it.

This app supports the listed Senate offices. The House Form Helper is a separate app. Offices may limit responses to constituents.

First open “share your opinion on legislation” for Cortez Masto, choose “Share your opinion or comments on bills or other issues” for Padilla, choose a topic on Murphy's page, or open “Share your Opinion” for Van Hollen. For Murphy, use that same topic in the helper or leave it to be chosen on the form. For Schatz and Booker, answer the response question yourself. Schatz's form does not have a subject field; Sanders's form has no subject field and limits comments to 2,000 characters. Markey's form has no phone field. Some forms do not offer every territory. For Warren, review and answer the required federal-agency question yourself. Slotkin's form opens on the Senate outreach site. If a prefix is unavailable, choose it manually. Filling sends your details to the form page. Review all fields, complete any CAPTCHA, and submit yourself. The helper never submits forms.

Shaheen and Merkley have no subject field; select Merkley's country on the form. Fetterman's subject is a topic selection rather than a text field, and its message limit is 1,200 characters. Ossoff's form combines both address lines into one field. Review response and newsletter choices yourself.

Import an export to begin.
