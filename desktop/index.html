Senate Form Helper

Imports the contact export from the website. Your details stay in memory while this app is open. The export file remains on disk until you delete it.

The helper supports Senators Gillibrand, King, Cortez Masto, Padilla, and Murphy. Other offices are not yet supported. Offices may limit responses to constituents.

First open “share your opinion on legislation” for Cortez Masto, choose “Share your opinion or comments on bills or other issues” for Padilla, or choose a topic on Murphy's page. For Murphy, use that same topic in the helper or leave it to be chosen on the form. If a prefix is unavailable, choose it manually. Filling sends your details to the form page. Review all fields, complete any CAPTCHA, and submit yourself. The helper never submits forms.

Import an export to begin.
