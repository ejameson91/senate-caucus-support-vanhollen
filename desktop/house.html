House Form Helper

Imports the House contact export from the website. Your details stay in memory while this app is open. The export file remains on disk until you delete it.

The helper currently supports Representative Pete Aguilar. Other House forms are not yet supported for autofill. Offices may limit responses to constituents.

Manually answer the required response question. The helper does not change the newsletter or speech options. Review all fields, complete any CAPTCHA, and submit yourself. The helper never submits forms.

Import a House export to begin.
